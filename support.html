Purchases

  • Premium does not show on your iPhone or iPad: check that you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with, then Settings → Premium → Restore purchases.
  • To redeem a code: Settings → Premium → Have a code? Redeem it.
  • To change or cancel your subscription: Settings → Premium → Manage subscription, or Apple's guide.
  • Refunds are made by Apple: request a refund from Apple.
