Voxen — Privacy Policy

The app

The app collects no personal data and shares none. The files you import, the text and audio made from them, your reading data, your settings and your Premium status stay in the app's private storage on your device. All of it is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases are made through the App Store, and Apple delivers the voice packs; Apple processes the data this needs under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/). The app learns from the App Store, on your device, only whether Premium is active and whether the free trial is open to you.

If you e-mail us

We use your address and your message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our interest is answering you). Writing to us is voluntary. Our e-mail provider, Google, receives the messages and may process them in the USA, for which an EU adequacy decision exists (EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework). We delete the correspondence once your request is settled, unless tax or commercial law requires us to keep it.

This website

GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts these pages. When you open them, GitHub receives your IP address and logs it for security purposes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our interest is a secure website), for as long as GitHub's privacy statement describes (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). GitHub may process it in the USA, for which an EU adequacy decision exists (EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify or erase your data and to restrict its processing, and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

You may object at any time to our use of your e-mail (Art. 21 GDPR).
